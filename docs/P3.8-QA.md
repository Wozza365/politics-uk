## Manual Flows

1. Start a new campaign. Confirm the clock begins running after the loading screen.
2. Pause from the clock control, change speed, resume, and confirm the state label changes.
3. Load an existing save. Confirm the campaign opens paused with "Restored campaign paused".
4. Open and close Menu, Targeting, Saved games, Help, and Explanation surfaces with keyboard only.
5. Tab through each open panel. Focus must remain inside the panel, Escape closes it, and focus
   returns to the triggering control.
6. Resolve a pending event choice from the feed. Confirm the clock cannot resume until the choice is
   made, then resumes only if no player/menu/election pause is still active.
7. Select a campaign target from the Targeting list without using the map. Confirm the action can be
   launched and recorded.
8. At 320 CSS px wide, confirm the feed, clock controls, map, hemicycle, and view switcher remain
   reachable without clipped dialogs.
9. At 200% text zoom, confirm modal bodies scroll and buttons remain visible.
10. With reduced motion enabled, confirm the clock indicator and global transitions do not animate
    unnecessarily.
11. With a screen reader smoke test, confirm the clock state, latest polling summary, dialogs, and
    panel labels are announced.
