### P4.4 - Hemicycle, charts, stats, and data-visualisation polish `DONE`

**Goal.** Upgrade the game's political data displays so they feel precise, readable, and premium:
hemicycle, house view, polling history, stat tiles, compass, objective status, and party breakdowns.

**Depends on.** P4.0 and P4.2. Should coordinate with P4.3 for shared colour and overlay semantics.

**Steps:**

1. Define a chart theme for ECharts/custom SVG: background, axis/grid style, label colour,
   tooltip style, party-series treatment, trend deltas, uncertainty/estimated-data treatment, and
   reduced-motion behaviour.
2. Refine the hemicycle and house views: dot sizing, spacing, group ordering, subtle depth,
   selected-party emphasis, hover/press states, legend, seats-per-dot explanation, and expanded
   breakdown layout. Keep the SVG responsive and stable.
3. Add non-colour identity helpers to party-heavy visuals: labels, initials, patterns, symbol chips,
   or consistent party marks from P4.6. Ensure Northern Ireland grouping, independents, Speaker,
   and local parties remain distinguishable.
4. Upgrade stat tiles for polling, seats, money, membership, councils, mayoralties, leader approval,
   days since election, and objectives. Use tabular numerals, consistent delta arrows/icons,
   provenance indicators, and compact sparkline or trend treatment where useful.
5. Polish `PollingHistoryChart` and `CompassView` with the new visual language. Compass positions
   should remain mathematically honest while looking less placeholder-like.
6. Improve dense lists and breakdowns: selected seats, decisive places, campaign objectives, arcs,
   consequences, save summaries, and contest cards. They should scan cleanly without nesting heavy
   cards inside heavy cards.
7. Add text alternatives and summary rows for charts so the data remains understandable without
   relying on the visual alone.

**Files:** `src/components/HemicycleView.vue`, `PollingHistoryChart.vue`, `CompassView.vue`,
`PartyStatCard.vue`, `GoalStatusStrip.vue`, `CampaignJournal.vue`, `ContestCard.vue`,
`ExplanationDetails.vue`, result-screen breakdown components, shared chart/theme helpers, and
tests where rendering logic changes.

**Acceptance:** polling, seats, money, objectives, and seat breakdowns are faster to scan than the
Phase 3 versions and feel visually cohesive. Data displays use tabular numbers, meaningful
non-colour cues, accessible labels/summaries, and screenshot-verified responsive layouts.
