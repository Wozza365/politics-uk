### P4.1 - Premium title, setup, loading, and result screens `DONE`

**Goal.** Make the outer game loop feel like a finished game: title, continue/load/new-game setup,
loading, restore, and result screens should have atmosphere, hierarchy, and confidence without
becoming a marketing landing page.

**Depends on.** P4.0 tokens and art direction.

**Steps:**

1. Redesign the title screen around the product identity: a full-screen civic/map-room backdrop,
   strong "Politics UK" title treatment, concise menu actions, visible continue context, and an
   unobtrusive save-format/version detail. Avoid floating hero cards and generic centred stacks.
2. Upgrade the new-game flow with a richer scenario briefing: date/timeline control, objective
   preview, difficulty signal, selected-party summary, and campaign horizon arranged for scanning.
   The first viewport should communicate "this is a political strategy game" through map, chamber,
   or campaign imagery.
3. Rework party selection cards with premium visual hierarchy: party accent rail or banner,
   leader/mark area, polling/seats/difficulty data, selected/hover/focus states, and non-colour
   selection cues. Keep cards dense but not cramped.
4. Improve load/restore screens with save-slot thumbnails or compact campaign summaries where
   feasible: selected party, date, objective state, last updated, and compatibility state. Error
   states should feel recoverable and polished.
5. Replace the generic loading spinner with a branded loading treatment: map trace, chamber dots,
   or campaign-document motif. Respect reduced motion and keep loading copy short.
6. Redesign the result screen as a dramatic but legible election-night summary: outcome headline,
   seat total, majority threshold, decisive places, model explanation link, restart/load actions,
   and optional visual reveal sequence from P4.5.
7. Verify all screens at 320px width, 200% text zoom, desktop, and high-contrast mode. Check that
   party names, save summaries, and long dates do not overflow buttons/cards.

**Files:** `src/screens/TitleScreen.vue`, `NewGameScreen.vue`, `LoadGameScreen.vue`,
`LoadingScreen.vue`, `RestoreScreen.vue`, `ResultScreen.vue`, `src/components/PartyCard.vue`,
shared primitives from P4.2 if available, `src/style.css`, and relevant tests.

**Acceptance:** starting, continuing, loading, restoring, and finishing a campaign feel visually
coherent with the new art direction. The screens use shared tokens, have no clipped text at narrow
viewports, preserve keyboard operation, and include before/after screenshots in the Phase 4 QA
notes.
