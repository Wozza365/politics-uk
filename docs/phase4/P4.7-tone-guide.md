# P4.7 Tone Guide

Completed on 2026-07-01.

## Voice

- Sober, sharp, election-night credible.
- Concise first; drama only when stakes are genuinely high.
- Describe simulation output as modelled campaign state, not real-world reporting or prediction.
- Avoid jokes, memes, defamatory wording, or unsupported claims about real people.

## Event Feed

- Lead with scope, severity, date, and status.
- Headlines should say what changed; body/effect text should explain why it matters.
- Action labels should describe the player choice, not a vague command.
- Resolved entries should preserve the action taken and the recorded effect.

## Explanations

- Treat explanations as an audit trail.
- Separate recorded choices, direct effects, background drift, and estimated data.
- Use model/provenance language for uncertainty.

## Formatting

- Dates use en-GB short dates.
- Money, polling, seats, and deltas should be compact enough for HUD cards.
- Long text should truncate only on labels, never on decisive effects or warnings.
