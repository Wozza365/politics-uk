## Art Direction

North star: modern civic command room. Politics UK should feel like a live election operations desk:
precise, institutional, tense, documentary, and data-rich. It should borrow the discipline of a map
room, the credibility of public-record tables, and the finish of premium strategy-game HUDs.

Reject:

- Generic SaaS dashboards, especially big rounded cards floating on a dark grey page.
- Toy-like party colour floods. Party colour means ownership or comparison, not decoration.
- Decorative gradients that do not carry information.
- Oversized hero typography inside dense tools.
- Cards inside cards except for repeated items, dialogs, or genuinely framed tools.

## Semantic Tokens

The CSS contract starts in [`src/style.css`](../../src/style.css) with `--puk-*` variables and
Tailwind v4 theme aliases. Later P4 tasks should consume these by semantic role rather than by raw
colour.

Colour roles:

- `--puk-color-app-bg`: deep ink app background.
- `--puk-color-map-backdrop`: map staging field, separate from app chrome.
- `--puk-color-surface` and `--puk-color-surface-raised`: HUD panels and elevated panels.
- `--puk-color-border` and `--puk-color-border-subtle`: 1px structural lines.
- `--puk-color-text`, `--puk-color-text-muted`, `--puk-color-text-disabled`: text hierarchy.
- `--puk-color-player-focus`: active player focus, selected controls, keyboard focus.
- `--puk-color-opponent`: opponent activity.
- `--puk-color-contest`: contest or live electoral pressure.
- `--puk-color-success`, `--puk-color-warning`, `--puk-color-danger`: outcome/risk states.
- `--puk-color-data-neutral`: neutral chart/grid/reference data.
- `--puk-color-institutional` and `--puk-color-premium-accent`: Westminster anchor and scarce
  brass accent.
- Party accent bridge variables define how much party colour may tint surfaces and borders.

Contrast notes on dark surfaces:

- Text on app/surface is 16.72:1 / 16.45:1.
- Muted text on surface is 10.10:1.
- Player focus on app background is 8.81:1.
- Warning, danger, success, and data neutral on app/surface are all above 6.8:1.
- Disabled text is 4.05:1 on surface and must not carry essential state or data.
- When party colours sit on dark surfaces, use them as swatches, borders, or mixed accents. If a
  party colour becomes text, it must be checked against 4.5:1 or paired with a neutral text label.

## Type And Numbers

Use the system UI stack through `--puk-font-ui`; reserve a later font swap for a dedicated asset
decision. Use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` for dates, polling, deltas, money, countdowns,
seat counts, and chart labels. Prefer `en-GB` dates: `1 January 2025` in clocks and `2 Jan 2025` in
dense logs. Deltas always show a sign except zero, money uses compact GBP labels in panels, and
seat counts use whole numbers with thousands separators only when needed.

Uppercase labels are allowed only for tiny section labels, table headings, and status chips. Panel
titles stay small and dense; hero-scale type belongs only to title/setup/result screens.

## Surfaces, Layout, And Motion

Surfaces use 6-8px radii, 1px borders, and one of three elevations: raised panel, panel overlay, or
modal. Fixed controls use `--puk-control-height-sm`, `--puk-control-height-md`, and
`--puk-control-icon` so labels and icons cannot resize the HUD. Z-index roles are map 10, HUD 20,
popover 35, dialog 50, and toast 60.

Spacing uses the 4px scale in `--puk-space-*`. Dialogs stay within the viewport with a max height
and internal scrolling. Sheets and menus pause the clock through existing menu state, and should
avoid nesting extra cards unless the child represents a repeated row/item.

Motion tokens: 120ms for control feedback, 180ms for panels, 320ms for map snap, and 520ms for
result reveals. Use `--puk-ease-standard` for routine UI and `--puk-ease-emphasized` for map/result
moments. Reduced motion keeps fades and state changes but removes travel, snap, and flourish.

## Assets

Use `lucide-vue-next` for shared iconography once P4.6 adds it. Party marks should begin as
documented placeholder monograms/swatches, not unofficial logos. Portrait placeholders should feel
like editorial cuttings or parliamentary archive silhouettes, with licensing notes beside any real
asset. Background imagery should be generated or rights-cleared and should show civic interiors,
maps, papers, broadcast monitors, or campaign rooms rather than abstract gradients. Favicon/app icon
direction: a crisp ballot/map-room mark using institutional green plus a restrained brass accent.
